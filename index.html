Dear Tania,

Although I cannot express things using the proper language, I will give it a try in English. You entered my life in such an unexpected way. You turned out to be a presence which I would always seek in each moment of silence, in each ordinary day, and in each of my thoughts.

What makes you more than special to me is the fact that being around you brings light into everything. On those days when life becomes hard, just thinking about you makes it all better. Your smile has this quality that touches me so deeply, and your words are like a warm caress, bringing me comfort. You are among the most beautiful reasons why I am happy.

I don't care what others think of you or anything. It only matters how much you mean to me. I created this especially for you so that you will realize that you are very significant to me in ways that can't be explained.

You are a source of comfort amidst silence, a place of peace during chaos, and the type of individual whose affection is a gift that I feel fortunate enough to receive. Each time your thoughts cross my mind, I have the impression that life has granted me something invaluable - something beautiful and delicate that I could never let go of.

While there may be times when I might not express my feelings clearly, believe this with all my heart: you are significant to me. You are important to me. You are someone who makes ordinary things turn into treasured memories. Whatever the future holds for us, I just hope that at some point in this world, someone's heart will be made more beautiful because of you.

Always yours, Rahat
